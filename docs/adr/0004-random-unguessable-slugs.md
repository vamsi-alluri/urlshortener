# Random unguessable Slugs

Slugs are random base62 characters, at an initial length of 7 (~3.5 trillion combinations), not a sequential counter or a destination hash. On a public service, a guessable scheme lets anyone enumerate every Short Link and Destination that strangers have created — random Slugs make enumeration impossible and need no coordination between instances, and collisions (including against Deactivated Slugs) are rejected and retried, never reused. The cost is slightly longer Slugs; the length can grow later but never shrink, so the initial length of 7 is the real commitment.
